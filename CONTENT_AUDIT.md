## Enquiry delivery

The original form uses its own Gravity Forms endpoint and reCAPTCHA. The redesign does not impersonate that endpoint. It validates the supplied fields and prepares a reviewable email draft to elenah@boussias.cy. The user opens their email app and sends it themselves. No personal data is stored or transmitted by the site. A direct link to the organiser’s original online form is supplied alongside it.

## Verification

JavaScript syntax and local asset references checked. Confirmed unique IDs, section anchors, input labels, current speaker data, source-matching complete topic and sponsor statements. Checked countdown before, at and after its deadline; conditional form fields; encoded draft data; explicit opt-in handling; and the review/edit flow. Browser visual QA was not requested or performed.
